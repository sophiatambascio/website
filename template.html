Getting Started

Who Can Join?

Anyone can gain access to BTU. Our goal is to provide the most diverse, inclusive environment for makers of all types in a highly experimental and productive space.

Due to increased campus security, BTU Lab is currently unable to sponsor BuffOne Guest cards.

How It Works

New members attend a 20-minute general orientation covering community expectations, safety, and equipment.

When to Come In

The orientation schedule and all scheduled events are posted on the BTU Lab Calendar. Some equipment, including the wood shop and laser cutters, requires its own separate orientation — see the Wood Shop and Lasers pages for details.

Materials Policy

BTU Lab is not a free supply store. But there are many ways to barter, including displaying your work in BTU, tagging the lab in social media, offering workshops, or volunteering to organize supplies. If you need something, give something in return.
